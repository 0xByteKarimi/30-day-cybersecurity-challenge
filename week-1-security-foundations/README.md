## 🎯 What This Week Is About

Before you can stop an attack, you need to understand it. Week 1 builds your foundation in cybersecurity — the vocabulary, the mindset, and the core ideas every defender needs.

By the end of this week, you will know:

- Why cybersecurity matters in the real world
- How attackers think and what they target
- The CIA Triad (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability)
- How to protect your own accounts with strong passwords and MFA
- The basics of hashing and encryption
- Where to find free Cisco learning resources
- Which Cisco certification path fits you
