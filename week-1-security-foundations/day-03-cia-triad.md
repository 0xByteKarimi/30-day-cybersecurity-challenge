## 🎯 Focus

The CIA Triad — Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability.

---

## 📚 Learned

I studied the **CIA Triad**, the three core goals of every security program:

- **Confidentiality** — only the right people can see the data
- **Integrity** — the data is accurate and has not been tampered with
- **Availability** — the data and systems are there when needed

🔗 [Principles of the CIA Triad](https://learningnetwork.cisco.com)

---

## 🏢 Real-World Examples From My Own Org

### Confidentiality
**Example:** Only HR can see employee salary records. Everyone else is blocked.

**Why it matters:** If salaries leaked, it would cause distrust and legal problems.

### Integrity
**Example:** Financial reports are locked after approval. Any change leaves an audit trail.

**Why it matters:** If numbers could be changed silently, decisions would be based on lies.

### Availability
**Example:** The email server must stay online during business hours. Backups and failover keep it running.

**Why it matters:** If email goes down, no one can work.

---

## 💡 Key Takeaways

- Every security decision balances these three goals.
- Too much confidentiality can hurt availability (people cannot access what they need).
- Too much availability can hurt confidentiality (everyone can access everything).
- Good security finds the right balance for the business.
