## Phishing email red flags
Found one in my inbox. Red flags I noticed:

1. **Urgent language** — "Act now or your account will be locked."
2. **Wrong sender address** — looked like my bank but the domain was misspelled.
3. **Generic greeting** — "Dear Customer" instead of my name.
4. **Suspicious link** — hovered over it, went to a random site, not the bank.
5. **Spelling mistakes** — small typos throughout the email.

## Key takeaways
- Phishing is the most common way attackers get in.
- Urgency is a tactic. Real companies do not rush you.
- Always hover over links before clicking.
- When in doubt, go to the site directly instead of using the link.
