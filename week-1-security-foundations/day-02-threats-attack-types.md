## 🎣 Phishing Email Red Flags

I found one in my inbox. Here is what gave it away:

### 1. Urgent language
"Act now or your account will be locked."

### 2. Wrong sender address
Looked like my bank, but the domain was misspelled.

### 3. Generic greeting
"Dear Customer" instead of my name.

### 4. Suspicious link
Hovered over it — went to a random site, not the bank.

### 5. Spelling mistakes
Small typos throughout the email.

---

## 💡 Key Takeaways

- Phishing is the most common way attackers get in.
- Urgency is a tactic. Real companies do not rush you.
- Always hover over links before clicking.
- When in doubt, go to the site directly instead of using the link.
