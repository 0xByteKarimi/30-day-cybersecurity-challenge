## ✅ Three Threats That Surprised Me

### 1. Social engineering
**What it is:** Tricking people into giving away information.

**Why it surprised me:** I thought hacking was mostly technical. Turns out it is often just talking to people.

### 2. Ransomware
**What it is:** Malware that locks your files until you pay.

**Why it surprised me:** It can hit hospitals and schools, not just big companies.

### 3. Insider threats
**What it is:** Attacks from people inside the company.

**Why it surprised me:** I always assumed threats came from outside.

---

## 💡 Key Takeaways

- Most breaches start with a human mistake.
- Cybersecurity is not just about technology. It is about people and process too.
- Small businesses are targets too, not just large ones.
