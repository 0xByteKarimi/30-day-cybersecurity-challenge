`7 days` · `~20–60 min per day` · `Hands-on`

## 🎯 What This Week Is About

Week 1 gave you the mindset. Week 2 gives you the hands-on skills to protect what matters. Attackers go after networks and endpoints first — this week shows you how to defend them.

By the end of this week, you will know:

- How networks are segmented and defended
- Why defense in depth matters
- How to assess and harden endpoints
- How to review access and apply least privilege
- What makes Wi-Fi and mobile devices risky
- How to write and test Access Control Lists (ACLs)

## 💡 How to Use This Week

1. **Build the lab.** Days 8, 11, and 14 rely on Cisco Modeling Labs. Set it up early.
2. **Take notes on what breaks.** Troubleshooting is where the real learning happens.
3. **Audit your own setup.** Use your own Wi-Fi, laptop, and folders for practice.
4. **Commit with `Day X`.** The tracker updates itself.
5. **Do not skip the ACL testing.** Order matters — and you will only learn why by breaking it.

---

## ⚡ For Certification Holders

The **Stretch** task for Week 2 is a full security review of your home lab or small network.

Document:
- Password policy
- MFA status
- Guest Wi-Fi isolation
- Software update status
- Firewall settings
- Device inventory

Then identify **three improvements you can implement immediately**.
